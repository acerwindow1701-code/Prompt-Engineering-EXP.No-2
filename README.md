EX-02-Cross-Platform-Prompting-Evaluating-Diverse-Techniques-in-AI-Powered-Text-Summarization

AIM:

To evaluate and compare the effectiveness of prompting techniques (zero-shot, few-shot, chain-of-thought, role-based) across different AI platforms (e.g., ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Copilot) in a specific task: text summarization.

SCENARIO:

You are part of a content curation team for an educational platform that delivers quick summaries of research papers to undergraduate students. Your task is to summarize a 500-word technical article on "The Basics of Blockchain Technology" using multiple AI platforms and prompting strategies.

Your goal is to determine which combination of prompting technique + platform provides the best summary in terms of:

1.Accuracy

2.Coherence

3.Simplicity

4.Speed

5.User experience

OUTPUT:

Artificial Intelligence platforms are widely used for generating summaries of technical content. In this experiment, different prompting strategies were tested on multiple AI platforms to summarize an article titled “The Basics of Blockchain Technology.” The goal was to identify which combination of AI platform and prompting technique produces the best summary for undergraduate students.

The platforms used in this experiment were:

OpenAI ChatGPT
Google Gemini
Anthropic Claude
Microsoft Copilot

1. Zero-shot Prompting

In zero-shot prompting, the AI is directly asked to summarize the article without any examples or additional guidance. This method is fast and simple but may sometimes miss important details.

2. Few-shot Prompting

Few-shot prompting provides sample summaries before asking the AI to generate a new summary. This improves consistency and formatting quality.

3. Chain-of-Thought Prompting

In this technique, the AI is instructed to think step by step before generating the final summary. It improves reasoning, technical accuracy, and coherence.

4. Role-based Prompting

The AI is assigned a specific role, such as a teacher or professor. This helps the model generate simpler and more user-friendly summaries suitable for students.

The generated summaries were evaluated based on the following criteria:

Accuracy
Coherence
Simplicity
Speed
User Experience

After comparing all outputs, it was observed that role-based prompting on ChatGPT produced the most understandable and balanced summaries. Chain-of-thought prompting on Claude provided highly accurate technical summaries, while Gemini performed best in terms of speed.

RESULT:
The experiment showed that prompting techniques significantly influence the quality of AI-generated summaries. Among all combinations, ChatGPT with Role-based Prompting produced the best overall summary in terms of accuracy, coherence, simplicity, and user experience.
